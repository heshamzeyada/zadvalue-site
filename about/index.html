About ZadValue

Experts in adding value

ZadValue is a Canadian consulting firm, built on a hand-picked team of healthcare and life sciences experts brought together around one goal: to add value for our partners.

Mission

To drive sustainable growth and market leadership for our partners, helping organizations unlock their potential and reach their goals.

Vision

To be the most trusted consulting partner for life sciences and healthcare companies entering and growing across the GCC and MENA.

Positioning

A Canadian consultancy that combines Western regulatory fluency with decades of on-the-ground Gulf healthcare experience.

Who we are

A focused team for emerging and specialty healthcare

A hand-picked team with 150+ years of combined experience, strong in pharmaceutical management, GCC markets and global healthcare standards.

  • Pharmaceutical, biotech, MedTech and medical nutrition focus
  • Former directors and managers from the regional industry
  • Market access, regulatory affairs and business development
  • Based in Canada, active in the GCC and wider MENA
