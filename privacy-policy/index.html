Privacy policy

How ZadValue Inc collects, uses and protects personal information. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

ZadValue Inc is a Canadian consulting company based in Bedford, Halifax, Nova Scotia. Our website address is https://zadvalue.com. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@zadvalue.com.

What we collect

  • Information you send us through the contact form or by email: your name, company, email address and message.
  • Basic technical information that our web server records automatically, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time of the request.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and to arrange meetings or provide services you ask about.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run our email, hosting and website, and where the law requires it. Some providers may store information outside Canada.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to respond and to keep business records. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect information.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law you may ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@zadvalue.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
